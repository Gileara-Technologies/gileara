## 1. What This Strategy Is (And Is Not)

This document is a **communication framework**, not a product spec, engineering plan, or brand design file. It exists to ensure every message Gileara produces — on the site (`gileara.org`), in proposals (`service-catalogue.md`), in discovery conversations (`Strategy/web-ui-upgrade-plan.md` §Booking flow), in insights posts (`gileara.org/insights`), in case studies (reframed as "How We Transform"), and in internal notes — aligns with:

- The **canonical company identity** (`Company/gileara-company-profile.md`).
- The **service catalogue and economic engine** (`Company/service-catalogue.md`: 5 packages, 3 tiers, USD pricing, recurring revenue from $85/mo to $1,360/mo, managed services embedded at every stage).
- The **consultancy positioning** (`Strategy/tech-wing-positioning.md`: digital transformation consultancy, not software-development vendor; three pillars — Digital Operations Platform / Automation & Integration / Managed Services & DevOps).
- The **strategic readiness framework** (`Strategy/technology-readiness-roadmap.md` and `service-catalogue-readiness-plan.md`: do not announce as deliverable anything that has not passed its readiness gate; message intent honestly, claim results cautiously).
- The **visual and verbal identity** (`Strategy/gileara-visual-system.md`: premium editorial consultancy aesthetic; typography IS design; numbered pillars; Gileara Velocity tokens; component-level mapping to vault knowledge).
- The **website repositioning plan** (`Strategy/web-ui-upgrade-plan.md`: reposition from "software agency" to "digital transformation consultancy for MSMEs in emerging markets, piloting in Ghana"; phased-honest launch — pricing transparent; scenarios labeled; no fabricated results).

**Excluded from this strategy:** anything in `NonCompany/` (personal projects, unrelated real estate, academic coursework, side ventures). See `Company/gileara-company-profile.md` §11 — "What Gileara Is NOT."

## 3. Positioning & What Gileara Is (And Is Not)

### 3.1 The Consultancy Stance

Gileara is **not** a software-development vendor, a freelance shop, or a generic SaaS. It is a **digital transformation consultancy** delivering recurring-revenue packages with managed services embedded (`Company/gileara-company-profile.md` §Company Identity; `Strategy/tech-wing-positioning.md` §Thesis).

This shapes communication in practical ways:

- **Sell business results, not software hours.** Proposals reference business outcomes (revenue growth, cost reduction, process speed) and use package names (`Digital Foundation`, `Business Operations`, `Customer Growth`, `Business Intelligence`, `Automation & Efficiency`) rather than technology stacks (`Next.js`, `n8n`, `BI dashboards`) in client-facing messaging (`Strategy/web-ui-upgrade-plan.md` §Services page shape; `Strategy/gileara-visual-system.md` §CapabilityPanel).
- **Talk about operations, not just builds.** Every message includes managed services language (IT support, backups, security monitoring, SLAs) because retention is the economic engine (`Strategy/tech-wing-positioning.md` §Key Corrections #1; `Strategy/gileara-visual-system.md` §MetricBlock mapping to MRR).
- **Avoid "launch" language without "run" language.** The site Approach section states: **Diagnose → Implement → Run (managed) → Grow** (`Strategy/web-ui-upgrade-plan.md` §Approach). No message stops at deployment.

### 3.2 Ideological Guardrails (Message Integrity)

`Company/gileara-company-profile.md` §11 defines what Gileara is **not**. These guardrails prevent message drift:

- Not a hobby / personal project space.
- Not a university / academic coursework archive.
- Not an unrelated-industry consultancy (e.g., luxury real estate unless it's an MSME digital transformation client).
- Not a generic software agency selling development hours or big-enterprise banking/regulatory software.
- Not the sole vehicle for internal AI/agent infrastructure (operator tooling ≠ client work).

Any proposed message — whether a site section, insight post, proposal text, or pitch — must pass this filter.

## 6. Brand Voice & Tone

From `Strategy/gileara-visual-system.md` (§Brand Thesis, Typography, Component Library) and `Strategy/web-ui-upgrade-plan.md` (§Design language evolution, Message architecture):

### 6.1 The Voice Mandate

> **Premium editorial consultancy — not generic SaaS; not freelance craft; strategic, platform-first, outcome-focused.**

This is an aesthetic and verbal mandate together. It means:

- **Typography IS design.** The display font (`IBM Plex Sans` for display per `D22`; previously `Hanken Grotesk` / `Geist` / `Manrope`) is treated as a graphic object; body (`Inter`, 18–22px, generous line-height 1.6) is readable at distance; meta (`JetBrains Mono`, 12–14px uppercase) handles labels and dates. Voice mimics this: clear, authoritative, editorial — not casual.
- **Asymmetrical composition.** Numbered pillars (`01` / `02` / `03`) organize ideas. Sections alternate light (`#F7F7F3` surface) and dark (`#101010` surface-dark) rhythm. Voice alternates between confident declaration (light sections) and deep proof (dark sections).
- **Motion is choreography, not decoration.** Reveals are slow (`300–600ms`, ease-out), staggered (`120ms` between cards), scroll-triggered. Voice mirrors this: messages build progressively; no abrupt pivots; no bounce effects.
- **No gradients; restrained radius.** `radius-4`, `radius-8`, `radius-12`, `radius-16` — never universal pill. Voice avoids hyperbole and universal claims. It uses specific, bounded statements (`$550` → `$16,000+`; `Basic / Professional / Enterprise`; `rolling out Q4 2026`).

### 6.2 Voice Attributes (Derived from Design Tokens)

Mapping `gileara-visual-system.md` tokens to verbal behavior:

| Token / Design choice | Voice behavior | Example in message |
|---|---|---|
| `surface` (`#F7F7F3` off-white) | Light, open, welcoming | "Your customers are already online." (Hero — invitation, not demand) |
| `surface-dark` (`#101010` near-black) | Deep proof, serious claim, technical authority | "Every package includes IT support, updates, backups, security monitoring, SLA-backed response." (Approach / Managed services) |
| `ink` (`#111111` black) | Primary statement; direct; no ambiguity | "We build the systems your business runs on." |
| `ink-secondary` (`#6F6F6F`) | Caption, meta, qualification | "Managed from day one — tier-based. Dedicated engineer at Enterprise." |
| `accent` (`#00BFA5` Gileara teal; `#44DDC1` glow) | Call-to-action; growth signal; outcome metric | "Book a Free Consultation"; metric numbers (`$1,360`); "Available" / "Rolling out" status chips |
| `Geist` / `Manrope` / `IBM Plex Sans` display | Confident headline; oversized; tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) | "From spreadsheets to a business that scales." |
| `Inter` body | Readable explanation; generous line-height; 18–22px | Package descriptions, FAQ answers, insight body text |
| `JetBrains Mono` meta | Technical credibility; precise; uppercase tracking (`+0.04em`) | Tier names, prices (`$550` / `$85/mo`), dates (`2026-08`), package numbers (`01` / `02` / `03`) |

### 6.3 Voice Rules (Non-Negotiable)

1. **Always name the outcome before the technology.** "Intelligent business management" before "inventory module"; "customer pipeline" before "CRM"; "automated approval flow" before "n8n workflow."
2. **Always pair a feature claim with a managed-services claim.** Every feature description includes the tier-based service promise (`IT Support & Updates` · `Backups` · `Security Monitoring` · `SLA Support` · `Dedicated Engineer`).
3. **Always state exclusions.** Proposals and content clearly list what's not included (domain purchase, hosting, photography, advertising spend, content creation, hardware procurement) (`service-catalogue.md` §Exclusions per package; `web-ui-upgrade-plan.md` §D1 — full transparency).
4. **Never invent results.** Case studies / "How We Transform" use `status: scenario | pilot | case-study` labels; outcomes are goals, not claimed results; metrics slots are for real proof only (`D4`, `D9`, `D5`).
5. **Always reference the readiness principle.** Marketing communicates intent honestly; delivery claims only what has passed its readiness gate (`service-catalogue-readiness-plan.md` §Readiness Checklist; `web-ui-upgrade-plan.md` §D5).
6. **Never name a third-party platform as a feature.** We don't market other companies' tools for them. When a channel matters to the client (chat apps, mobile money), name the business behavior it enables ("orders taken over chat", "reconciled payments") — never the vendor's product. Market fluency is shown through the pains we name, not the tools we list (`tech-wing-positioning.md` §Key Corrections; `gileara-company-profile.md` §ICP).
7. **Always use USD pricing in every public message.** 1 USD = GHS 11.05 (Bank of Ghana interbank, Aug 2026); 20% operational uplift absorbed; no hidden add-ons (`service-catalogue.md` §Pricing Notes; `gileara-company-profile.md` §Economic Engine).
