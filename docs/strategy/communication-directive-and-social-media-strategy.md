# Gileara Communication Directive & Social Media Strategy

> *"We build the systems your business runs on."*

---

## Part 1 — Communication Directive

### 1.1 Purpose

This document is the **single authoritative reference** for how Gileara Technologies communicates — internally, externally, and across every channel. It aligns *who we are* with *what we say* and *how we say it*.

Every piece of communication — a social media post, a proposal, a discovery call, a website section, an insight article, or an internal note — must trace back to this directive.

### 1.2 Who We Are (Identity Statement)

| Element             | Statement                                                                                              |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **What**            | A digital transformation consultancy                                                                   |
| **For whom**        | Ghana's Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs)                                                    |
| **What we deliver** | Business outcomes — not software, not code, not hours                                                  |
| **How** | Modular technology packages built for how business actually runs, with managed services from day one |
| **Positioning** | "The technology partner helping MSMEs in emerging markets become efficient, digital, and scalable businesses — headquartered in Ghana." |
.
### 1.3 What We Are NOT (Non-Negotiable Exclusions)

Per `Company/gileara-company-profile.md` §11:

- Not a software agency selling development hours
- Not a freelance shop or generic SaaS vendor
- Not a hobby, personal project, or academic space
- Not an unrelated-industry consultancy
- Not big-enterprise banking/regulatory software

**Every message must pass this filter. If it could be said by a generic software agency, it is the wrong message.**

### 1.4 The Five Signature Phrases

These are **non-negotiable, always-consistent** across all content:

| # | Phrase | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "We build the systems your business runs on." | Hero statements, pitch openings, social media bio, email signatures |
| 2 | "The technology partner helping MSMEs in emerging markets become efficient, digital, and scalable businesses — headquartered in Ghana." | Positioning statements, about pages, partnership decks |
| 3 | "Outcomes over code." | Internal culture, proposals, discovery calls, thought leadership |
| 4 | "Managed from day one." | Every package discussion, proposals, FAQ, pricing conversations |
| 5 | "Built for how business actually runs." | Market proof, mobile-first/low-bandwidth builds, local credibility |

### 1.5 Communication Principles

| Principle            | Rule                                                                                                                              |       |                                                  |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Outcome first**    | Always name the business result before the technology. "Intelligent business management" before "inventory module."               |       |                                                  |
| **Managed always**   | Every package message includes the managed services promise (IT support, updates, backups, security, SLA, dedicated engineering). |       |                                                  |
| **Honest by design** | Never claim results before documented evidence. Status chips for unready packages (`rolling out Q4 2026`).                        |       |                                                  |
| **Ghana-proof**      | We never market another company's tool. Market fluency is shown through the business pains we name and the behaviors the system enables — never through listing third-party platforms. Mobile-first, offline-tolerant, low-bandwidth builds are proof. |
| **USD transparent**  | All public pricing in USD. 1 USD = GHS 11.05 (BoG interbank, Aug 2026). No hidden add-ons.                                        |       |                                                  |
| **No fabrication**   | Case studies use `status: scenario                                                                                                | pilot | case-study`. Outcomes are goals, never invented. |

## Part 2 — The Narrative Framework

### 2.1 The Three-Act Story

Every communication follows this arc — on the website, in a pitch, in a social post, or in a discovery call:

```
PAIN → SOLUTION → OUTCOME
```

| Act          | The Message                                                                                                                                                | Example (Social Media)                                                                                                 |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **PAIN**     | MSMEs run on notebooks, scattered spreadsheets, orders spread across chat threads, and long reconciliation. This is not a technology gap — it is a growth ceiling.          | "Still running your shop from notebooks? Your competitors aren't."                                                     |
| **SOLUTION** | Gileara builds the digital systems the business runs on — mobile-first, offline-tolerant and low-bandwidth by design, with managed services from day one. | "We build the system your business runs on: sales, stock, customers — reconciled, in one place, managed from day one." |
| **OUTCOME**  | The business becomes efficient, digital, and scalable — growing from a $550 foundation into a $16,000+ transformation client with full managed support.    | "From spreadsheets to a business that scales. Starting at $550."                                                       |

| **Restaurant / Bar / Canteen**           | Manual orders, reconciliation at close, orders lost across chat threads | Automated orders, approvals, inventory    | Fewer errors, faster service          |

| **Distributor / Trade**                  | Multi-branch chaos, no API integration, manual reconciliation   | Enterprise operations, API, multi-branch  | Scalable distribution, real-time data |

## Part 3 — Considerations

### 3.1 Pre-Launch Considerations

| Consideration                  | Status                                         | Action                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Readiness gating**           | Packages 1–3 in development; 4–5 planned       | Use status chips (`available` / `rolling out Q4 2026`). Never claim delivery before readiness gate passes.    |
| **Case study gap**             | No live clients yet                            | Use `scenario` status. Frame as "How We Transform" — outcomes as goals, not results. Invite founding clients. |
| **Website as Package 1 proof** | Site is dual-role: marketing + reference build | Every page must demonstrate the quality of what we deliver. The site IS the portfolio.                        |
| **Social proof vacuum**        | No testimonials, no metrics, no logos          | Lead with insight content, not social proof. Build proof through quality content first.                       |
| **Founder credibility**        | Rodney Hagan — CTO, technical depth            | Use founder story as authority signal. "Built by engineers who understand MSME reality."                      |
| **Budget for social**          | Phase 1: organic only, no ad spend             | Content-first strategy. Allocate ad spend only after first 3–5 live clients generate real proof.              |
| **Ghana-market trust**         | New brand in a market with scam fatigue        | Lead with transparency. Published pricing. Real phone number. Real people. Face-to-face discovery calls.    |

### 3.2 Message Integrity Guardrails

| Guardrail                    | Rule                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **No invented metrics**      | Never claim "increased revenue by X%" without documented evidence                                   |
| **No unready features**      | If a package is `rolling out`, say so — in content and on the site                                  |
| **No generic SaaS language** | Never use "revolutionary", "world-class", "cutting-edge", "game-changing" — these are not our voice |
| **No tech-first messaging**  | Never lead with "AI", "n8n", "Next.js", "PostgreSQL" in external content — lead with outcomes       |
| **No hidden costs**          | Every public message includes what's managed and what's excluded                                    |
| **No mission drift**         | Every message passes the "What Gileara Is NOT" filter                                               |

### 3.3 Audience Considerations

| Factor | Implication |
|---|---|
| **Language** | English primary (Ghana business language). Optional Twi captions for social media video content. |
| **Platform reality** | Customers operate from chat threads and phone calls. Enable the communication behaviors they already use — but never advertise a third-party tool as our feature. |
| **Payment trust** | Trust comes from transparent all-inclusive pricing and delivered outcomes — never from naming a payment brand. Talk about reconciled payments, not payment vendors. |
| **Device** | Content must be mobile-first. 80%+ of MSME owners in emerging markets consume content on phones. |
| **Connectivity** | Content should load fast. Avoid heavy video unless on Wi-Fi-only platforms (YouTube). |
| **Decision cycle** | MSME owners decide fast but verify through relationships. Discovery calls > long sales funnels. |

## Part 4 — Social Media Campaign Strategy

### 4.1 Campaign Positioning

**Primary platforms:** LinkedIn (B2B authority), Instagram (visual proof + MSME lifestyle), Facebook (Ghana MSME community), and direct chat/mobile for engagement (never advertised as a feature — used as the human channel it is)

**Social media bio (all platforms):**
> The technology partner helping MSMEs in emerging markets become efficient, digital, and scalable businesses. Managed from day one. Built for how business actually runs.

**Link:** `gileara.org`

### 4.2 Content Pillars

Every social media post falls into one of five content pillars, each mapped to a package and a business outcome:

| Pillar | Package | Content Focus | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01 — Digital Presence** | Digital Foundation | Website tips, Google Business Profile, online trust signals, SEO basics for MSMEs | 2x/week |
| **02 — Operations** | Business Operations | Inventory tips, sales tracking, expense management, "spreadsheet vs system" comparisons | 3x/week |
| **03 — Customer Growth** | Customer Growth | CRM tips, loyalty programmes, customer-messaging strategy, repeat business strategies | 2x/week |
| **04 — Data & Decisions** | Business Intelligence | Dashboard examples, financial literacy, reporting tips, "what your numbers are telling you" | 1x/week |
| **05 — Automation** | Automation & Efficiency | Workflow tips, time savings, approval systems, "automate this, not that" | 1x/week |
| **06 — Gileara Voice** | Company / Brand | Founder insights, behind-the-scenes, Ghana-market commentary, company milestones | 1–2x/week |

### 4.3 Content Formats

| Format | Platform | Purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Carousel / Slide post** | Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook | Educate — "5 signs your business outgrew spreadsheets" | Pain–solution format with Gileara brand design |
| **Short-form video (Reel / TikTok / Shorts)** | Instagram, YouTube, TikTok | Show — screen recordings, before/after, founder talking head | "30 seconds: What your sales look like on Gileara vs. a notebook" |
| **Text post / Thread** | LinkedIn, X (Twitter) | Thought leadership — founder voice, industry commentary | "Why 'digital transformation' fails for MSMEs (and what works)" |
| **Story / Status** | Instagram Stories, Facebook Stories | Daily — behind-the-scenes, quick tips, polls | "What's your biggest operations headache?" (poll) |
| **Long-form article** | LinkedIn Articles, gileara.org/insights | Authority — deep-dive on a package topic | "What 'all-inclusive' should mean when you buy software" |
| **Case scenario** | All platforms | Social proof (honest) — "How We Transform" vertical stories | "A pharmacy in Labone: from paper records to secure digital systems" (scenario status) |

### 4.7 Engagement Strategy

| Tactic | Execution |
|---|---|
| **Comment on MSME content** | Follow and engage with Ghana MSME accounts, small business pages, industry associations. Add value, not pitches. |
| **Own community group**   | Create a "Gileara MSME Insights" community group for tips, not sales. Build trust first. |
| **Discovery call CTA** | Every post ends with a soft CTA: "Free discovery call — link in bio" or phone number. |
| **Founder DMs** | Rodney personally responds to LinkedIn DMs and Instagram inquiries. High-touch, not automated. |
| **User-generated content** | Once live clients exist: encourage them to share their Gileara dashboard screenshots (with permission). Frame as "real businesses, real systems." |
| **Polls and questions** | Weekly Instagram/LinkedIn polls on operations pain points. Use results to shape content. |

### 4.8 Launch Sequence (First 90 Days)

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Content Volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1 — Seed** | 1–4 | Establish brand presence. Publish bio, profile, 8–12 foundational posts across pillars. Founder voice posts to build credibility. | 8–12 posts/week total |
| **Phase 2 — Educate** | 5–8 | Content-heavy. Daily posting. Carousel and video formats dominate. Build the "go-to" authority for MSME digital transformation in Ghana. | 10–14 posts/week total |
| **Phase 3 — Engage** | 9–12 | Community building. Community group launch. Polls, questions, DM engagement. Begin replying to Ghana business news/events. | 10–14 posts/week + engagement |
| **Phase 4 — Convert** | 13+ | Introduce case scenarios (pilot status). Founding client invitations. Direct discovery call CTAs in every post. Begin retargeting if ad budget approved. | 10–12 posts/week + conversion focus |

### 4.9 Paid Social (Future — Phase 4+)

Only activate after:
- 3–5 live clients generate real proof
- Website conversion path is tested and optimised
- Organic content has established baseline engagement

**When ready:**
- **LinkedIn Ads:** Sponsored posts targeting Ghana-based business owners, MSME decision-makers, industry associations
- **Instagram/Facebook Ads:** Retargeting website visitors; carousel ads for package spotlights
- **Budget allocation:** 70% retargeting, 30% prospecting
- **Creative:** Repurpose top-performing organic content (proven engagement)

### 4.10 KPIs & Measurement

| Metric | Phase 1–2 Target | Phase 3–4 Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Follower growth** | 500+ across platforms | 2,000+ |
| **Engagement rate** | 3–5% per post | 5–8% per post |
| **Website traffic from social** | 200+ monthly visits | 1,000+ monthly visits |
| **Discovery calls booked** | 2–4/week | 5–10/week |
| **Inbound inquiries** | 50+ | 200+ |
| **Content saves / shares** | Track top formats | Double previous quarter |
